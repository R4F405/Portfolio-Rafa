Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Data controller: Rafa San Pablo González

Contact email: sanpablorafa@gmail.com

Overall purpose: to present a personal portfolio and to answer messages sent to the owner

1. Summary

This site does not collect personal data from visitors. There are no forms, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Personal data is only processed when someone chooses to send an email, and then only in order to reply.

2. What data is processed and why

Situation Data Legal basis Retention
You email the owner Name, email address and the contents of your message Your consent, given by initiating contact (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) For as long as the conversation lasts plus a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted
You visit the site Technical logs kept by the hosting provider, which may include IP address, date and browser Legitimate interest in keeping the service running and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) As determined by the hosting provider's own retention periods

No profiling takes place, there are no automated decisions, no special categories of data are processed and no data is collected for advertising purposes.

3. Browser storage

The site does not use cookies. It stores two preferences in the browser's local storage. Neither is sent to any server, and neither identifies anyone:

  • portfolio-locale: the language you picked, so it is not asked again.
  • portfolio-soundsEnabled: whether interface sounds are on or muted.

As they are strictly necessary to provide the service you requested, they are exempt from the consent requirement under Article 22(2) of the Spanish LSSI-CE. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

4. Recipients and processors

No data is sold or disclosed to third parties. Only the following providers are involved, as they are needed for the site and the email account to work:

  • GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft), hosting the site through GitHub Pages. It processes the technical logs described above.
  • Google Ireland Ltd., as the email provider used to receive and answer messages.

Both may process data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and, failing that, on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. Third-party links

The site links to external profiles and repositories (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram and project websites). Following those links takes you off this site and subjects you to each platform's own privacy policy, over which the owner has no control. There are no embedded social widgets loading third-party content on their own.

6. Published screenshots

Projects are illustrated with screenshots of test environments or with mockups. The names, companies, amounts and documents shown in them are fictional or anonymised: they do not correspond to real people or identifiable clients.

7. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to sanpablorafa@gmail.com. You will receive a reply within one month.

If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

8. Children

The site is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you become aware of such a case, please write to the contact address so the data can be deleted.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. It is a static site with no database and no admin panel, which keeps the attack surface to a minimum. Emails are kept in the owner's account, protected by a password and two-step verification.

10. Changes to this policy

If the site ever adds forms, analytics or any other kind of processing, this policy will be updated before that goes live. The version in force is the one published here, bearing the date shown above.
